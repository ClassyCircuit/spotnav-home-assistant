## What the existing system actually does

The custom HA panel owns auth, charger context, polling, WebSocket calls, permission/generation epochs and actions in `frontend/sidebar/panel.js`; `adapter.js` translates dashboard and Morek status into the shared `mountDashboard` renderer. The fixture preview uses that renderer, but the current host bar holds real actions and several safety/status messages outside it. Consequently screenshot parity of the existing preview alone does not verify the live controls. The current page has vehicle, plan editing, price insights, chart, execution evidence, history and four separately saved advanced groups. A new shell must migrate these real responsibilities and expose preview callbacks without duplicating action admission or simulating success.

Plan data is not just bars: `adapter.js` validates `window_details` against proposal period timestamps, total planned energy and, where priced, total cost. Its per-window cost/energy is exposed only when these checks pass; the price derived from cost/energy is configured variable cost, not necessarily raw spot. Stale or historical proposals are suppressed. `dashboard.js` currently feeds validated windows to the proposal track while sent, effective and measured tracks have separate origins and coverage. Existing price/day and history/day navigation are independent; timestamps align the tracks, not an assumed shared day. Chart bands are absolute EUR c/kWh, with neutral fallback when conversion is not trusted; soft-cap line requires comparable basis and revision. These semantics and the chart's time-axis alignment are part of the product, not decorative legacy.

`charge-session.js` distinguishes fresh measured flow from reported charging, unmatched transaction, pending request, paused, offline, fault and uncertain outcome. `panel.js` imposes freshness, ownership and action gates; Charge Now is admin-confirmed and uses a fixed end, and timeout can leave an unknown result rather than cancelling a server operation. Stage 19 adds recoverable resume and provenance/recovery states beyond the older audit. Existing settings use compare-and-set revisions and independent drafts; the older inventory identifies a UI/backend mismatch for non-admin cap/minimum/reserve edits, so verify current permissions and prevent presenting those edits as authorized. `reconcile.js`, draft maps, chart disposal and polling preserve in-progress interaction; replacing markup without preserving these lifecycles is a regression.

History is evidence-limited: detail retention is not seven days of guaranteed minute samples; spot costs and savings have basis and coverage, and late unplaced energy must not be attributed to a day. Historical spot costs are separate from forecast configured-variable costs. The archived roadmap's Stage 14 stability, Stage 15 evidence/learning and Stages 16–19 continuity/recovery all remain backend invariants, not tasks to rebuild. Current local tests and acceptance are not proof that the installed charger behaves this way.

## Assessment and required changes to the proposal

1. **Scope:** §§2, 4 and 13 say Overview only and leave other views unchanged. User has explicitly superseded this: redesign the whole custom sidebar, including all its current settings, history, insights, diagnostics and recovery, with no retained old sidebar UI. The legacy Lovelace card is not needed and its exclusive capabilities need not migrate. The prototype includes initial Explore, History and Settings layouts, but several controls are explanatory placeholders rather than complete working editors. Extend and finish these designs with current real workflows.
2. **Reference integrity:** The actual v4 prototype contains four navigable views and interactive controls, although many functions use fictional data or informational placeholders. The newer Overview has a richer blue visual layer than the earlier Explore/History/Settings designs. Treat all as design references, not production logic; apply the newer visual language consistently across all four. The screenshots include a simulated HA sidebar: HA already owns that navigation, but SpotNav still needs its own clear, persistent navigation between its four views.
3. **Hero art:** User chose the white 2022 BMW iX3 and Morek only. The demo embeds one clean scene with a connected cable and two smaller images with a baked-in 64% battery. Use the clean scene when suitable to ship (user decision), subject to asset provenance and state-correct disconnected/unknown treatment; never use the baked-in battery images as dynamic data. Obtain or adapt suitable art; do not assert flow through decorative motion. Avoid making asset creation or an unverified realistic cable a dependency for basic truthful text and controls. SOC and target are separate from the car's own configured limit.
4. **Chart and schedule:** Add a first-class list of the complete current validated proposal, but retain proposal/sent/effective/measured tracks and their unknown coverage. List/chart selection must use stable proposal context plus exact timestamps; it must not merge windows or join readback to proposal by time. Provide a day-jump when the row is outside the displayed range and clear stale selection on changed proposal. The proposed 7-day tab cannot imply seven days of available data; keep existing bounded navigation and display coverage. No fabricated cost or price, and do not call configured-variable averages spot.
5. **Information hierarchy:** Consolidate duplicated banners into a primary situation and contextual details while ensuring offline, uncertain, blocked, user pause, protection and recovery cannot be hidden by a prettier reassuring headline. Keep remediation and who acts next visible. System evidence tiles must not form an all-green progress ladder: each evidence type has independent timestamps, coverage and unknown states. Separate market red from fault red with labels and shapes.
6. **Workflows:** Replace visual controls, not controllers. Preserve Pause/Resume, Charge Now, fixed end, Return to Auto, existing confirmations and all permission/freshness/unknown gates. Preserve independent settings drafts, conflict/reload behavior, setup links and admin tools. A client timeout is not an action cancellation. Do not adopt prototype local-state toggles.
7. **Data and costs:** The mock's power, SOC, price, target, windows and costs are fictional. Use actual source quality and timestamp; unknown is not zero. Show current plan cost basis distinct from history's measured-energy spot cost and its day-average spot savings baseline. Hide or qualify data whose producer or unit is unproven, and never invent a range estimate or numeric SOC input.
8. **Responsive and accessibility:** The proposal's sizes, keyboard/touch access, focus restoration and reduced-motion are good requirements, and the supplied mobile screenshot illustrates Overview only; it does not verify the other three screens. Design complete mobile states for all screens, including dense history/settings, not only Overview. Maintain 44px targets, no page overflow, accessible chart/list selection and adequate contrast. Actual browser/visual acceptance remains unrun until authorized under the current fast-test restriction.
9. **Architecture and staging:** The suggested frontend model is illustrative, not an API schema. Reuse existing decoders and formatting helpers; a small view model can organize structured state while leaving backend decisions authoritative. Each migration substage must wire a visible vertical slice and keep live/preview aligned; final cleanup must remove superseded sidebar UI. No new backend features, and no new API solely for a mock field. Restrict changes to existing backend if required to preserve an existing fact or behavior.

## Four-tab design and current-data mapping

- **Navigation:** four plainly named destinations — Overview, Explore, History, Settings — always easy to reach. The mock has top tab buttons and an Overview-specific mobile menu; its screenshot's HA rail is host UI, not the product's navigation. Use top navigation on desktop and persistent bottom navigation on mobile (user decision), preserve tab, scroll and in-progress drafts across routine refresh and navigation, and keep critical warnings reachable on every tab.
- **Overview:** the newer layer shows a cinematic hero with SOC/target, situation, proposed next window and action buttons; price chart, recent charging, strategy, price perspective, system evidence and freshness. The detailed design additionally requires a complete linked proposal-window list absent from the Overview reference screenshot. Show live conditions rather than mock status and reconcile the visual with real actions, per-source ages and plan quality. The mock's “all systems normal” cannot be a generic green state when evidence is unknown.
- **Explore:** market opportunity, current/lowest/plan price, cost construction, price availability and a cheapest upcoming period. Map published days and price insights to existing APIs, retain chart units/coverage and the independent plan track. Mock spot/variable toggle, hourly aggregation, cost breakdown and selected-plan coverage are not automatically available as equivalent production values: verify actual feed and precision; omit or clearly mark unavailable where the existing backend doesn't provide a trustworthy result. Price insights' contiguous cheapest period is not the planner's selected windows.
- **History:** session list, observed energy, spot-only cost, savings and coverage, with example monthly graph. Map to `get_charging_history`/`get_sessions` and current day/month controllers; preserve real evidence retention, differing session/day attribution and unplaced energy. Do not promise a complete monthly profile or SOC start/end where no underlying measurement exists. Preserve explicit gaps, excluded energy and comparable kWh.
- **Settings:** the mock's six groups correspond broadly to charging goal, vehicle source, market/costs, charger parameters, notifications and system diagnostics. Replace placeholder review buttons with the existing real forms and their separate save/draft/conflict and permission rules. Theme following and localization in the demo are labelled proposals, not existing backend features or approved new features. Distinguish current settings from unsaved default suggestions.

Across tabs, shared status and action state must have one source, while user choices such as chart zoom and history day remain local and survive tab switches. A tab change is not a replan or charger action. Prototype demo scenario switches, synthesized bars and browser-local edits are not production functionality.

## Remaining limits

This is a source/design assessment, not a visual approval, a complete audit of every backend branch, a deployed-state investigation, or proof of physical charging. Precise accessible layout, image asset provenance, and mobile visual quality require implementation and user-directed review. Stage 19 coordinator review remains independent of this design stage. The user has settled the Lovelace scope and BMW/Morek art direction; do not reopen them as product gates.
